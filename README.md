## project Overview
the project is about creating a website for a business of my choice and i chose a business of my own.House Of Dimpho is We are a beauty and hair care businesses the focuses on helping clients look and feel beautiful. We offer services such as braiding, nails and other beauty treatments. The business started from my passion for beauty and helping people feel good about themselves. House Of Dimpho was started in 2021 as a garage-based business in Rustenburg then moved to Pretoria in 2024, where it was still home based. Word of mouth and social media helped expose the business work which helped it grow and relocate to a dedicated studio space, where if offers a full range of beauty services including braiding, nails and eyelashes. 
## website goals and objectives
### Mission 
The mission of House Of Dimpho is to help every client look and feel beautiful and confident through personalised and affordable beauty care in a warm and welcoming environment.
### Vision
Our vision as a business is to be successful and a trusted business that helps empower people to feel confident, beautiful and comfortable in their own skin. We aim to provide high quality and professional beauty services by creating a welcoming environment for every client.
